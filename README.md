# MultiModal RAG

Question answering over recorded tech talks. Answers cite the exact moment in the video.

Status: in progress.

## Corpus

Eight FOSDEM 2026 talks, listed in [corpus.csv](corpus.csv) with their video and slide URLs. The media files are not stored in this repo.

The recordings are licensed under [CC BY 2.0 BE](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/be/deed.en). They are © their speakers (named in `corpus.csv`) and [FOSDEM](https://fosdem.org). Each talk's `event_url` links to its source page.
